When we approach a publisher for a guest post, they usually provide their set of guidelines and requirements first. Based on those guidelines, in most cases, we can confirm whether the content is likely to be accepted before proceeding.

However, even if we adapt the content according to their guidelines and submit it for final approval, the publisher still has the right to reject it. Rejection can happen due to the content quality, issues with the quality or relevance of the link/site being promoted, competitor-related concerns, or other editorial reasons.

This is the usual guest post process we follow: check the publisher’s guidelines → prepare/adapt the content → submit for approval → make any required changes → publish if approved.
